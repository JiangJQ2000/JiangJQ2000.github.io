01 / WHO?

ABOUT
ME

I'm Jùn-Qián Jiāng (alternative romanization: Chün-Ch'ien Chiang), a cosmologist and postdoctoral researcher at the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (KASI) in Daejeon.

I was born in Wenling, Zhejiang, in 2000, where the first rays of sunlight reached mainland China that year. I speak Wu, Mandarin and English.

My research interests span a broad range of topics in cosmology and gravitational waves, from theory to observation. I am particularly interested in the cosmic microwave background (CMB), large-scale structure (LSS), pulsar timing arrays (PTAs), the dark sector, cosmological tensions, reconstruction, black holes, and computational approaches to cosmology.

Broadly speaking, my research focuses on two complementary directions: using cosmological observations to test fundamental physics, and conversely, exploring new physics to explain cosmological observations.
